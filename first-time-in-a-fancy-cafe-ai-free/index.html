What can I see without credits?

You can watch the public input and output examples without spending credits. They show a synthetic-person test and a golden retriever test. The free still-image preview, free video generation and signup credits are not currently available.

What would a video cost?

The current Standard 480p setting uses 20 credits for a 15-second video. One-time packs begin at $9.99 for 20 credits; see pricing for the other packs. Check the displayed price before completing a PayPal purchase.

How to explore the results

  1. Open the public examples and compare each input with its output.
  2. Choose Person or Pet in the generator and review the photo requirements.
  3. Check the current availability and the displayed credit cost before starting a video.

These examples are existing results, not a free preview of your own photo.

A free way to film the joke yourself

You can film an original version with a friend and safe props. Use a stable seat made to support a person; make it look like a table in the shot. Hold the camera still, let the regular reveal the correct use and keep the correction understated.

Free preview questions

Can I preview my own photo for free? No. The still-image preview is not currently available.

Can I watch a result without paying? Yes. The public examples can be watched without credits, but they do not predict the exact result for a different photo.

Is the full video free? No. The current 15-second Standard 480p setting uses 20 credits.

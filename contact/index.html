A payment or credit question

Include the account email, order reference, purchase time and pack name. A completed checkout can take time to be confirmed; check the order before buying again. Never send a full card number or security code.

Our refund policy explains payment review and generation credit returns.

Deletion, rights or corrections

Send the relevant page or task ID and describe the change you are requesting. For a factual correction, include a source we can review. For privacy requests, use the email associated with your account so we can verify ownership.

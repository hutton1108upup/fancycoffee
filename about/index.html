A familiar joke, with your face in it.

Fancy Cafe AI is an independent browser tool for making the first time in a fancy cafe POV scene from one Person or Pet photo.

In this guide
    Make your fancy cafe POVOpen the generator

    Your photo guides a character in a newly generated cafe scene. We use coarse depth layouts for composition and movement; original performer images are not used as a face-swap canvas. An omitted regular is a fictional adult. Results are AI-generated fiction, not a record of a real visit.

    The product

    Upload a photo for the first-timer on the table, and optionally one for the regular on the bench. The preset prepares a 15-second Standard 480p vertical MP4. It uses a fixed cafe scene; no prompt writing or voice cloning is involved. AI likeness, hands, motion and timing may vary.

    There is no free still preview or signup credit; visitors can watch the public examples without credits. Full video generation uses one-time credit packs.

    How your photos are processed

    The service adapts reference frames with SeeAPI’s Nano Banana image models and animates them with Seedance. This site may refer to Waffo in historical or test payment records. That reference does not mean the current generation flow uses Waffo Prompt Sift. Uploaded images and completed outputs undergo content checks. These checks do not verify identity or permission: use only photos you are entitled to edit.

    Your photos and intermediate frames are scheduled for deletion seven days after their last task. Completed videos are kept for 30 days. Files remain private to your account, and can be deleted sooner once related tasks finish. See the privacy policy.

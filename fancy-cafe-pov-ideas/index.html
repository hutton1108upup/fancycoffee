Solo: you are the first-timer

Choose the Person template and upload one authorized adult photo for the first-timer. The second character is generated by AI, not selected with another photo. A clear, well-lit face can help the model use the photo as a reference; the result is newly generated and may vary.

See the synthetic-person input and output example to understand what the test produced.

Pet: a golden retriever example

Choose the Pet template and upload one pet photo for the main character. A second animal is generated by AI; it cannot be chosen through a second upload. See the golden retriever input and output example. It is one tested result, so other animals and photos may produce different results.

Friends, family and work jokes

A friend who pretends to know every cafe or a new colleague following an office coffee expert can fit the setup. These are ideas for a filmed skit or caption: the current generator cannot cast a second uploaded person. Use photos with permission and avoid presenting an AI scene as a real incident.

What about a cat or dog?

The Pet template accepts a pet photo, and the public example shows one tested golden retriever result. Cat results have not been verified in our examples, so do not assume every species will follow the scene equally well. For a filmed version, use a safe, low prop instead of putting an animal on a wall-mounted table.

“Fancy coffee shop,” “cafe” and “café”

These captions can introduce the same joke. Keep the object and reaction easy to read instead of adding more plot. For a cafe’s own filmed version, ask permission, use safe seating props and avoid obstructing other guests.

Original caption ideas

  1. POV: I understood the menu. Not the furniture.
  2. First visit. Strong coffee. Questionable seating choices.
  3. Trying to look like a regular.
  4. I was just checking the table’s structural integrity.

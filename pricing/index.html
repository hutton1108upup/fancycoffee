Fancy Cafe AI pricing questions

How much does an Fancy Cafe AI video cost?+

At fancycafeaivideo.pro, each 15-second Standard 480p video uses 20 credits. A one-time pack starts at $9.99, with no subscription.

Is there a free preview or signup credit?+

There is no free still preview or signup credit. Full Standard 480p videos use 20 credits; packs start at $9.99.

What happens after I buy credits?+

Once payment is confirmed, the credits appear in your account. If you opened checkout from the generator, you return to your saved cast. Payment does not start a video automatically: you choose when to generate.

Do credits expire or renew automatically?+

Credit packs have no scheduled expiry and do not renew automatically. If a completed payment has not appeared in your balance, contact support with the order reference before buying again.

Do I get a refund if generation fails?+

Confirmed failed tasks return the credits they used. Tasks still processing or awaiting review need a status check first. Returning credits is different from a payment refund, which is reviewed separately under the refund policy.

Read the refund policy or contact support about an order. AI likeness and motion vary; check your completed MP4 before sharing.

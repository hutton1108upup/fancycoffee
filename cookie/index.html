Fancy Cafe AI: Cookies and local storage

Login cookies keep your Fancy Cafe AI account session active. Your browser can also store an unfinished photo and video settings for up to 24 hours so you can return after signing in or purchasing credits.

Signed-in drafts are separated by account. Browser site-data controls can clear these drafts and cookies; clearing login cookies signs you out. Google Identity Services displays the Google sign-in button and may show One Tap according to your browser and Google account settings. Google, external links and payment services have their own storage policies.

Google Analytics 4 uses analytics cookies to measure visits to public pages. Private account pages, photos and generated media are excluded. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled. You can block analytics or clear its cookies in your browser, or use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Microsoft Clarity uses cookies and similar technologies to understand interactions on public pages, including session recordings and heatmaps. See our privacy policy for details.

Read our privacy policy or contact support for more details.

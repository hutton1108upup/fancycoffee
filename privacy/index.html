Fancy Cafe AI Privacy Policy

Your photos and finished videos are private account files. Making a video requires sending selected photos to an AI provider, photos and generated video to a content-moderation provider, and private output to an audio-processing provider. This policy explains that processing, the records we keep and your choices.

This policy applies to Fancy Cafe AI at fancycafeaivideo.pro. For privacy questions or requests, email support@fancycafeaivideo.pro.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and sign-in information: your email, name, optional profile image, account identifiers, verification records and sessions. Sign-in records can include your IP address and browser information. This site uses Google sign-in; it does not offer email/password registration or store a Google password.
  • Photos and video tasks: uploaded photos, file names and sizes, your selected roles, duration and frame, task references and status, and generated videos. These records connect your private media to your account and generation history.
  • Credits and payments: credit purchases, usage, returned credits, payment references, amounts, currency and payment status. We do not collect or store your full payment card number.
  • Support messages: your reply address, messages and any references or attachments you choose to send about an account, video, payment or rights request.
  • Technical information: hosting and authentication services process request and device information to deliver the site, maintain sessions and investigate failures or abuse.

When Google sign-in is available and you choose it, Google supplies your account identifier, email, name and profile image. We do not receive your Google password or request access to your Gmail inbox.

2. How we use your information

We use Google-verified account information to sign you in and protect private files. Account recovery takes place through Google. We use selected photos and task settings to make the video you request, add its soundtrack, show its progress and make the completed file available for download.

Photos and generated video are also checked for prohibited content. The moderation result can stop a task or hold its output from delivery. Credit and payment records let us maintain your balance, confirm a purchase, return credits for a confirmed failed task and resolve billing questions. Support information is used to answer your request. Technical records help us maintain the service, investigate errors, prevent duplicate processing and address misuse.

We do not publish customer photos or generated videos in the public example gallery by default. We do not use uploaded photos to train our own AI model. This statement does not describe an external provider’s separate practices; review the services below before submitting a photo.

3. Services that process your information

A service receives information when its feature is enabled and you use it. An unavailable checkout, sign-in or generation feature does not send a task to that provider.

  • Cloudflare: hosts the website, database and private media storage, and delivers requests. Its infrastructure processes account records, uploaded files and generated files. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • SeeAPI: receives the selected photos through time-limited HTTPS links and instructions to create new cafe-scene frames with Nano Banana 2.1, then animate them with Seedance 2 Mini. The reference video guides scene timing; the generated MP4 has generated audio. Its underlying model services may also process those materials. Selected photos are checked before generation, and the completed video is checked after audio processing and before delivery. We do not request returned frame images or turn your files into permanently public URLs for these checks. See the SeeAPI video generation documentation and content moderation documentation.
  • fal.ai: receives a time-limited link to the generated video and the soundtrack to combine them into the finished MP4. See the fal.ai Privacy Policy.
  • Google: processes the sign-in request when you choose Google login. We receive the basic identity information described above. The homepage loads Google Identity Services to show its standard sign-in button and, where your browser permits it, One Tap. Google receives the browser request even if you do not complete sign-in. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Support email: Cloudflare routes messages sent to our support address to our support mailbox in Gmail. These services process your message and any attachments. Account verification and password recovery emails are not sent by this site.
  • PayPal: processes payment information when PayPal checkout is enabled. We receive the order or transaction reference, status and amount needed to grant credits and review refunds. We do not receive your full card details, and your uploaded photos and generated videos are not sent to PayPal.
  • Waffo: may appear in historical or test payment records. In that limited context, it may process the payment reference and status needed to keep the record accurate; it does not receive your uploaded photos or generated videos.

These services may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Their processing locations, safeguards and retention practices depend on their services and policies. Deleting a file from this website does not, by itself, confirm deletion from a provider’s systems. We do not promise a specific deletion time for materials processed by SeeAPI or another external provider.

We may also disclose relevant information when required by law or when necessary to investigate fraud, protect someone’s safety or resolve a rights complaint. We do not sell customer photos or videos.

4. Photos and video access

Uploaded photos and generated videos are associated with your account. Viewing or downloading them through the site requires an authenticated account with permission to access the file. Providers receive time-limited links for the processing task. Those links allow access while they remain valid; do not share a private file or signed link unless you intend to give the recipient access.

The reference scene and credited public examples are separate public assets. They do not make your own uploads public. Only upload photos of adults who have agreed to the processing described here. Do not upload identity documents or unrelated sensitive information.

Access controls reduce unauthorized access, but no online storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Contact support if you believe your account or a private file has been accessed without your permission.

5. Storage and deletion

When generated files are available, the preview and its original frame are kept for 7 days after completion. Deleting the preview also queues its original frame for deletion. We retain a keyed hash of your Google account identifier to enforce the one-attempt limit, including after account deletion. This quota record contains no photo, email address or raw Google identifier.

Your unfinished photo and settings can be saved in this browser for up to 24 hours. Signed-in drafts are separated by account. Removing the selected photo clears it from the current draft; use your browser’s site-data controls to remove all locally stored drafts.

Uploaded photos and the intermediate images made from them are scheduled for automatic deletion 7 days after their last task finishes. A photo that has not been used in a task is scheduled for deletion 7 days after upload. Finished videos are kept for 30 days after completion. Download an MP4 you want to keep; deleting our stored copy does not delete copies you have downloaded.

Cleanup runs periodically. Files still needed for an unfinished task or a task awaiting a status check are kept until that task is resolved. Reusing a photo starts its retention period from the latest task’s completion. Files already stored when this policy takes effect receive a full 7-day photo or 30-day video retention period from activation. If a storage deletion fails, it remains queued for retry.

For a file or privacy request, use the Contact page. A photo needed by an unfinished task remains associated with that task until it is resolved; contact support if you need help with removal.

For account deletion or privacy requests, use the Contact page. Resolve any unfinished task first. Account deletion cleans up your private uploads, intermediate media and finished videos, then removes your account and sign-in records. If storage cleanup is temporarily unavailable, the request stays pending and you can retry account deletion. Contact support if a task or deletion request needs help.

Task references, credit transactions, payment records and support correspondence may need to be retained to resolve an outstanding task, prevent duplicate credits, investigate a dispute or meet an applicable recordkeeping obligation. For a removal request, we will explain any data that must be retained and the reason. External providers apply their own retention rules to the copies they process.

6. Cookies and external media

Sign-in uses session cookies. The site can also store display or language preferences in your browser. Blocking or clearing session cookies can sign you out or prevent access to account features.

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand visits to public pages and the sites that refer visitors. Google processes page views, browser and device information, and analytics cookies. We do not send your name, email, uploaded photos, generated media or private account-page activity to Analytics. Page addresses are stripped of query strings and fragments; external referrers are limited to their site origin. Advertising personalization and Google signals are disabled.

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use public pages, including interactions, session recordings and heatmaps. Microsoft may process device, browser and usage information and use cookies. The page content is marked for masking, and Clarity is not loaded on private account pages or addresses with query strings or fragments. Learn more in the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

You can block analytics through your browser or use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. Read how Google uses data from sites that use its services. Operational request records also help us run the service and investigate errors.

The homepage reference video is served by this website. Links to original performances may open YouTube, TikTok or other external sites. Those services may receive your IP address and browser information and use their own cookies or storage. Opening an external source link also takes you to a service governed by its own privacy policy.

An email link opens your chosen email application. A message is sent only when you send it. Support emails are processed by the email services used to deliver and receive them.

7. Your choices and requests

You can update available profile information, review your video history, remove stored files and delete your account through account settings. You can also request access, correction, a copy of your information or deletion by emailing support@fancycafeaivideo.pro.

Send the request from your account email where possible. Describe the records or files and the action you need; do not send a password, sign-in code, full card number or identity document in the initial message. We may need to verify that you are entitled to the information before acting on the request.

Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, obtain a portable copy or raise a concern with your local data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise an applicable right or ask about a request decision. Stopping future uploads does not automatically cancel a task already sent to a provider; contact support about an active task.

8. Age requirements

The service is for people aged 18 or older and does not permit photos of minors. If you believe a child’s information or photo was submitted, contact support with the relevant file or task reference so we can investigate and arrange removal where appropriate.

9. Policy changes and contact

We will update the date above when this policy changes. If a new feature changes which information is processed or which provider receives it, its processing will be described before that feature is made available. Check this policy before submitting new material.

Questions about a photo, video or privacy request? Email support@fancycafeaivideo.pro or use the contact guidance. The Terms of Service describe permitted use; the Refund Policy describes credits and payment reviews.

How to make a fancy cafe AI video

  1. Watch the public examples and choose Person or Pet in the generator.
  2. Upload one clear, authorized adult photo or one pet photo. A second photo cannot be used to select the other character.
  3. Sign in and check your balance. A Standard 480p video requires 20 credits; there is no free still-image preview or signup credit.
  4. With sufficient credits, start the video. Check its status and download the completed MP4. If its status is uncertain, check the existing task before starting another.

Credit packs are available through PayPal. Review the displayed price and credit cost before purchasing or generating.

Which photos work?

Use one JPG, PNG or WebP image up to 10 MB. For Person, choose an adult photo with a clear face and even lighting; use your own image or an adult's photo with permission. For Pet, choose a clear photo of the animal. Different inputs may produce different results; the public examples include one synthetic-person and one golden retriever test.

Who plays which role?

One uploaded Person or Pet photo guides the main character sitting on the table. The second character is generated by AI and cannot be selected with a second photo. The video is newly generated from your input; it is not a frame-by-frame face swap, and results may vary.

Sound and posting your video

The workflow requests generated audio with one MP4. Check the completed result before posting; a separate silent download or the original trend song is not promised. Use audio you are allowed to share on your chosen platform.
